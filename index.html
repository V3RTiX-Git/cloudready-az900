CloudReady is an interactive AZ-900 practice companion designed to make cloud fundamentals feel less abstract. Every question includes an explanation so a wrong answer can still move your learning forward.

What it includes

  • Original multiple-choice practice questions across all three AZ-900 skill areas.
  • Immediate feedback and explanations after each answer.
  • Topic-level performance, local progress saving, and targeted revision suggestions.
  • A readiness estimate that clearly communicates its limitations.

Content and accuracy

Topics are organised around Microsoft's published AZ-900 skills measured as of July 20, 2026. Questions are independently written for practice and are not official exam questions. Always consult current Microsoft Learn documentation for authoritative guidance.
